Pure projection from `ProjectGraph` + `LayoutedGraph` to React Flow node/edge models (`projectGraph`): typed group/module/symbol nodes with parent-relative positions, edge data, description-box geometry, heat fields, and the shared `node-data` types (`ProjectedGraph`, `RFNode`, `RFEdgeT`). Also the deterministic group color palette and the text `projectGraphSummary`.
